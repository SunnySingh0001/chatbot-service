# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook summarises your rights and obligations as an employee.
All policies are effective from your first day of employment unless otherwise stated.

## Section 1.1: Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to **15 paid leave (PL) days** per calendar year.
Unused PL may be carried forward up to a maximum of **10 days** into the next year.
Sick leave is capped at **7 days per year**; a medical certificate is required for any
illness lasting more than 3 consecutive working days.
Apply for leave at least **2 working days in advance** via the HRMS portal (hr.acmecorp.example).
Emergency leave may be applied retroactively within 24 hours of return.

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement | Carry-Forward Cap | Notice Required |
|------------|--------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Paid Leave (PL) | 15 days       | 10 days max       | 2 working days  |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 7 days        | 0 days (lapses)   | Within 24 hours |

## Section 2.3: Work from Home Policy

Employees may work from home (WFH) up to **2 days per week**, subject to manager approval.
WFH is not available during the first **90 days** of employment or during active probation.
Employees must remain reachable on Slack during core hours **10 AM – 4 PM IST**.
Equipment costs and home internet expenses are **not reimbursed** under the WFH policy.
Team leads may mandate office presence on days with critical deliverables or client meetings.

## Section 3.1: Probation Period

All new hires undergo a **90-day probation period**.
During probation: 0 PL days, 3 sick days.
Confirmation of employment is subject to manager and HR review at the end of the period.
Probationers may be let go with 15 days notice or equivalent pay in lieu.

## Section 4.2: Notice and Separation Policy

Notice period requirements are strictly defined by tenure status.
Under Section 4.2: if probation, notice is 15 days, else 60 days for confirmed staff.
Notice periods run from the date of written resignation; verbal notice is not accepted.
Buyout of notice period is at the sole discretion of HR management.

## Section 5.1: Code of Conduct

All employees must maintain professional, respectful conduct toward colleagues, clients, and partners.
Harassment, discrimination, or workplace violence is strictly prohibited and may result in
immediate termination without severance.
Report violations to **hr@acmecorp.example** or the anonymous ethics hotline **1800-XXX-0001**.
Retaliation against anyone who files a report in good faith is itself a policy violation.

## Section 6.2: Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within **30 days** of incurrence.
Meals during travel: up to ₹1,500 per day. Domestic travel: economy class only.
Expenses above ₹5,000 require pre-approval from the department head.
Personal expenses mixed into business claims will result in disciplinary action.
