# Leave and Notice Separation Operating Policy

This operational policy directive governs workforce leave categories, separation timelines, and exit clearance sequences. Staff members may consult the relevant sections below for step-by-step guidance on leave requests, handover milestones, and clearance timelines.

All provisions detailed within this operational document represent standard procedural protocols established for workforce management. Staff are encouraged to review the respective guidelines when scheduling time off or initiating transitional departures.

---

## Section 1: Detailed Leave Entitlement Framework

Acme Corp recognizes the vital importance of work-life harmony, rest, and personal recovery. To maintain high organizational productivity while safeguarding employee well-being, the company establishes the following categorized leave entitlement framework. Every employee is allocated specific leave quotas based on employment classification, statutory compliance guidelines, and organizational tenure.

The following schedule delineates all sanctioned leave types, their baseline annual entitlements, carry-forward parameters into subsequent calendar cycles, and respective authorization workflows:

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement | Carry-Forward Policy | Approval Level |
|------------|--------------------|----------------------|----------------|
| Paid Leave (PL) | 15 working days | Maximum 10 days carry-forward into next year | Direct Reporting Manager |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 6 working days | 0 days (strictly lapses on 31st December) | Direct Reporting Manager |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 7 working days | 0 days (lapses at year end; medical cert required after 3 days) | Reporting Manager and HR |
| Bereavement Leave (BL) | 5 consecutive days | 0 days (non-cumulative; applicable per incident) | Direct Reporting Manager |
| Marriage Leave (ML) | 3 working days | 0 days (one-time grant during employment tenure) | Department Head Approval |
| Compensatory Off (Comp-Off) | As earned | Must be availed within 60 days of accrual | Project Delivery Lead |
| Paternity Leave (PL-P) | 10 working days | 0 days (valid within 6 months of childbirth or adoption) | HR Business Partner |
| Maternity Leave (ML-M) | 26 calendar weeks | Statutory benefit per applicable regulatory framework | HR Operations Lead |
| Adoption Leave (AL) | 12 calendar weeks | Applicable for child under 1 year of age | HR Operations Lead |
| Study Leave (STL) | Up to 30 days unpaid | Requires 24 months of completed company tenure | Business Unit Vice President |
| Sabbatical Leave (SAB) | Up to 90 days unpaid | Permitted only after 5 years continuous service | Executive Committee Approval |
| Relocation Leave (RL) | 3 working days | Provided for interstate business office transfers | People Operations Desk |

Leaves must be scheduled and submitted via the enterprise HRMS portal (hr.acmecorp.example/leaves). Unplanned emergency leaves require notification to the team manager within 24 hours of absence initiation.

## Section 2: Comprehensive Notice Period and Separation Protocols

The separation and notice duration framework establishes operational continuity, thorough knowledge transition, and orderly handover of institutional responsibilities upon employment termination or voluntary resignation. The company strictly enforces structured notice durations calibrated against employee status and organizational risk.

This comprehensive separation protocol governs every resignation, departure, and contractual conclusion across all corporate business units. When an employee tenders resignation through the internal workflow system, management reviews operational dependencies, ongoing deliverables, and transitional milestones to ensure project commitments remain unimpaired. 

The determination of notice obligations depends fundamentally on the employee's formal confirmation status within the organization:

If an employee is currently serving an active initial probation period, the mandatory separation notice period is strictly 15 calendar days with zero eligibility for buyout or waiver options.

Else, for all confirmed full-time staff across all departments, the mandatory standard separation notice period is 60 calendar days, subject to two specific statutory exceptions:

Exception 1: Under mutual separation covenants formally endorsed by the Business Unit Vice President, the standard notice period may be reduced down to an expedited minimum of 30 calendar days provided all transitional knowledge transfers are certified complete.

Exception 2: In substantiated critical medical emergencies validated by an independent medical examination panel, the notice period requirement may be modified to an abbreviated 14 calendar days upon executive approval.

During the active notice period, employees are strictly prohibited from availing paid annual leave or personal casual leave, except in documented cases of bereavement or medical hospitalization. Employees on notice must remain reachable on corporate communication channels, participate in daily handover briefings, complete comprehensive functional documentation, and facilitate knowledge exchange with successor personnel. Failure to fulfill the mandated notice period without authorized waiver shall result in forfeit of separation gratuity clearances, formal withholding of experience documentation, and potential recovery of replacement cost damages as permitted by employment agreements.

## Section 3: Standard Operational Procedure for Employee Exit Clearance

To guarantee systematic transfer of operational responsibilities and secure reclamation of intellectual and physical corporate property, all departing personnel must rigorously execute the six-step separation sequence. These interdependent procedural steps must be conducted sequentially to achieve final operational clearance:

1. Resignation Submission: The employee must log into the enterprise human resource portal (hr.acmecorp.example/exit) and lodge formal electronic resignation, triggering automated notifications to the immediate line manager and designated HR Business Partner.
2. Initial HRBP Consultation: Within 48 hours of electronic notification, the employee must attend a mandatory structured exit briefing with the HR Business Partner to review contractual notice terms, handover roadmaps, and statutory timelines.
3. Knowledge Transfer Dossier: The departing staff member must draft a comprehensive Knowledge Transfer (KT) dossier identifying all active repositories, architectural schematics, operational credentials, pending defect tickets, and client stakeholder contacts.
4. Functional Handover Certification: The employee must conduct minimum five dedicated KT review sessions with the nominated peer successor and secure a formal digital sign-off from the project delivery director.
5. Physical Asset Reclamation: On or before the final working afternoon, the employee must surrender all issued enterprise hardware including company laptops, cryptographic hardware tokens, building access smartcards, and storage media at the Floor 2 IT Service Desk.
6. Financial Settlement Clearance: The employee must secure final sign-offs across IT, facilities, and administration portals to enable the payroll finance team to disburse the Full and Final (F&F) settlement within exactly 30 calendar days.

## Section 4: Handover Quality Audits and Knowledge Preservation

A successful exit depends heavily upon the qualitative thoroughness of the knowledge handover. Department managers must conduct independent validation audits prior to approving milestone releases. Handover dossiers that omit critical documentation, test harness scripts, architecture decision logs, or credential rotation records will be formally rejected by the quality governance board.

When a dossier is rejected, the employee and manager must revise the transition artifacts within 3 business days. Continued non-compliance triggers administrative review and may delay the issuance of final employment relief certification.

## Section 5: Notice Period Dispute Resolution and Grievance Workflows

During the separation lifecycle, conflicts may arise regarding the duration of notice, feasibility of handover milestones, or applicability of buyout waivers. The organization provides dual formal grievance mechanisms tailored to address concerns raised by either employees or managerial personnel.

### Section 5.1: Notice Period Grievance Redressal for Employees

Employees who believe that notice period terms have been unfairly enforced or that transitional demands exceed contractual obligations may submit a formal written petition to the Employee Relations Council within 5 business days of the contested decision. The petition must delineate specific grievances, relevant correspondence, and proposed mitigations. The Council conducts a preliminary inquiry within 7 business days, convening an interview with the employee and relevant human resource representatives. The Council renders a binding final verdict regarding notice adjustment or expedited relief within 10 business days of initial filing.

### Section 5.2: Notice Period Grievance Redressal for Managers

Managers who determine that a departing team member is deliberately withholding critical project knowledge, refusing transitional sessions, or engaging in unauthorized absenteeism during notice may escalate the non-compliance to the Management Oversight Panel within 3 business days of the infraction. The manager must present detailed activity logs, missed milestones, and communication transcripts. The Panel reviews the operational evidence and is empowered to halt exit clearances, extend notice requirements within regulatory limits, or recommend legal recovery of operational losses. The Panel communicates its binding determination within 5 business days.

## Section 6: Governance and Post-Separation Property Rules

All departing personnel remain legally bound by proprietary invention covenants and standard non-disclosure agreements executed upon commencement of employment. Proprietary design schematics, internal codebase repositories, customer rosters, and internal operational directives must never be duplicated, exported, or transferred to personal devices or outside entities.

Furthermore, departing staff must uphold non-disparagement covenants, refraining from communicating unverified claims or harmful statements regarding organizational processes, departmental panels, or commercial partners across public social media or commercial forums. Any violation of post-separation obligations will be vigorously pursued through appropriate regulatory and civil judicial avenues.
