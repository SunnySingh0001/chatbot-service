# HR Benefits and Compensation — CONFIDENTIAL

> This document is restricted to HR personnel only.
> Do not distribute, print, or share contents with non-HR employees.

## Section 1.1: Salary Bands and CTC Ranges

Salary bands are reviewed and updated every April by the Compensation & Benefits team.
Official compensation guidelines for the L1/L2 band and higher engineering grades:

| Level | Title                        | CTC Range (per annum) |
|-------|------------------------------|-----------------------|
| L1    | Junior / Associate           | ₹5 – 8 LPA           |
| L2    | Mid-level                    | ₹8 – 14 LPA          |
| L3    | Senior                       | ₹14 – 22 LPA         |
| L4    | Lead / Tech Lead / Manager   | ₹22 – 35 LPA         |
| L5    | Principal / Senior Manager   | ₹35 – 55 LPA         |
| L6    | Director / Head of Function  | ₹55 – 90 LPA         |

Offers above band maximums require VP and CFO approval.
Band overlaps are intentional; placement within band is based on skills and market data.

## Section 2.2: Promotion Criteria

Promotions are assessed twice a year, aligned with the performance review cycle (March & September).

- **L1 → L2**: Minimum 18 months tenure + "Exceeds Expectations" in 2 consecutive review cycles.
- **L2 → L3**: Minimum 24 months tenure + demonstrated cross-team impact.
- **L3 → L4**: Minimum 30 months at L3 + people management or large-scope technical ownership.
- **L4+**: Reviewed individually by department head, HR leadership, and CEO.

All promotions must be approved by the department head and HR leadership before being communicated.
Promotions are communicated to employees by their HRBP during the month following approval.

## Section 3.1: Performance Review Process

Acme Corp conducts **bi-annual performance reviews** in March and September.
Each review consists of three components:
1. **Self-assessment** — employee rates their own performance against set goals.
2. **Peer feedback (360°)** — 3–5 peers chosen by the employee and approved by the manager.
3. **Manager evaluation** — final rating set by direct manager and calibrated with HRBP.

Final ratings:
| Rating               | Description                              |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Needs Improvement    | Performance below expectations           |
| Meets Expectations   | Fully delivers on role expectations      |
| Exceeds Expectations | Consistently goes above expectations     |
| Outstanding          | Exceptional impact, top 5% of cohort     |

Ratings determine hike percentage and promotion eligibility (see sections above).

## Section 4.3: Bonus Structure

Annual bonuses are disbursed in **April** along with the annual hike letter.
The bonus pool is **10–20% of total payroll**, determined by company financial performance.

Individual bonus percentage by rating:

| Rating               | Bonus % of CTC |
|----------------------|----------------|
| Needs Improvement    | 0%             |
| Meets Expectations   | 8%             |
| Exceeds Expectations | 15%            |
| Outstanding          | 20%            |

Employees must be on the rolls as of **31 March** to be eligible for the bonus.
Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for a bonus that cycle.

## Section 5.2: Health Insurance

All confirmed employees receive group health insurance with a sum insured of **₹5 lakhs per family**.
Coverage extends to spouse, two children, and parents/in-laws (floater policy).
Insurance is activated on the **first day after probation confirmation**.
Dental and vision are not included in the base plan but can be added at the employee's cost.
Claims are processed via the HR portal; contact **benefits@acmecorp.example** for queries.
